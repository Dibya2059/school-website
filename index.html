Our Story

Shree Bhanubhakta
Secondary School

Shree Bhanubhakta Secondary School was established in 2022 B.S. in the open field of Barbot, Mallarani, supported by local education enthusiasts and community workers.

Teaching was begun by three dedicated individuals — Khupram Sharma, Dilliraj Upadhyay (Kaila Pandit), and Mathavar Singh Rana (Halkara) — who built this institution from the ground up with community donations.

Today the school runs from Class 1 to 12 in both general and technical/vocational streams, with a 5-year master plan (2076–80 B.S.) underway to become a model school for rural Nepal.

Message from the Principal

A Word from Our Principal

"Education gives every individual the ability to accomplish tasks with ease and shapes human life with awareness and consciousness."

Our school provides an opportunity for students from low-income rural families to access quality education at very low cost — covering general subjects, humanities, and technical streams including agriculture and plant science.

We believe that education must meet the needs of the time and society. At Bhanubhakta, both general and technical streams are available from Class 9 to 12, ensuring every student can find their path.

We are committed to building a model school that stands as a beacon of educational excellence in rural Lumbini Province.

Our Vision

To become a leading model institution producing capable, responsible, and compassionate citizens who contribute meaningfully to Nepali society.

Our Mission

To provide quality general and technical education with a focus on knowledge, discipline, creativity, and holistic development for every student.

Our Values

Honesty, mutual respect, teamwork, discipline, and the relentless pursuit of excellence — in academics and in life.
